Founded November 1871 · Sioux City, Iowa

Family-Owned Midwest Manufacturing Across Two Plants.

Started on the corner of Main and Seventh streets in Sioux City, Iowa, Sioux City Foundry Co. has grown into a regional One-Stop Metal Shop operating Plant 1 in Sioux City and Plant 2 in South Sioux City, Nebraska.

Two-Plant Footprint

Iowa & Nebraska Operations

Our two campuses sit minutes apart across the Missouri River, connecting steel warehousing, reinforcing steel processing, contract manufacturing, and iron casting under one management team.
