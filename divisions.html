03 · Contract Manufacturing · Plant 1

Welding, Machining, Beam Coping & Fabricated Tanks

Contract Manufacturing brings together robotic and custom welding, our on-campus Machining Center, Voortman beam coping, and Sherman Tank fabricated tanks built to customer specifications.

  • Production, robotic, and custom industrial weldments
  • On-campus Machining Center: drilling, boring, milling, coping, and thread milling
  • Voortman Beamliner structural beam coping
  • Sherman Tank fabricated storage, process, diesel, water, and vacuum tanks built to customer spec
  • Home of Wine On the Wall stainless steel wine storage systems

04 · Metal Casting · Plant 2

Jobbing & Specialty Alloy Iron Foundry

Full-service Plant 2 jobbing foundry pouring standard and wear-resistant specialty irons from a few ounces up to roughly 3,500 pounds for industrial, agricultural, pumping, mining, and OEM customers, from short runs to production volumes.

  • Broad alloy portfolio: gray iron, ductile iron, austempered ductile (ADI), white iron, Ni-Hard, high-chrome, high-nickel, high-silicon ductile, and custom OEM chemistry formulas
  • Weight range from ounces up to approximately 3,500 lbs across 125,000+ sq ft of foundry space
  • Green sand (Hunter, Squeezer, Rotolift), No-Bake, Isocure, and 3D-printed sand molding with in-house pattern shop
  • In-house metallurgical lab, heat treating, blasting, grinding, and recycled certified scrap feedstock from Plant 1
  • Integrated access to SCF's on-campus Machining Center and Contract Manufacturing divisions to cut freight, vendor handoffs, and secondary processing costs
One-Stop Processing Advantage: Pour at Plant 2 and move castings directly across the river to our Plant 1 Machining Center or Contract Manufacturing floor for finish machining, weldments, and turnkey assemblies.
